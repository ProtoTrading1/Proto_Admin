# 2026-09-24

## 20:55 — WhatsApp CRM tab (WATI broadcasts, analytics, opt-outs)

Added a `whatsapp` admin section: a WhatsApp CRM for the customers who opted in
to WhatsApp, sending template broadcasts through the Proto WATI account, with
delivery/read/click analytics and an enforced opt-out list.

**Scope decision recorded.** `CLAUDE.md` previously banned all outgoing
WhatsApp to customers. The owner explicitly asked for this capability, so the
ban has been narrowed rather than ignored: consented template marketing is now
supported; WhatsApp order alerts, welcome messages and the Intercom relay stay
removed. `CLAUDE.md` records both the reinstatement and what remains banned.

**Consent rule** (the core of the change) — three conditions, all required, and
re-resolved server-side on every send including a hand-picked list:
`customers.accept_whatsapp = true`, a number that normalizes to a valid one, and
no row in `whatsapp_opt_outs`. `api/_whatsapp-audience.js` is the single gate;
a phone list from the browser filters the eligible set and can never widen it,
so a stale tab cannot reach someone who opted out minutes earlier. The same
resolver backs the Contacts screen and the dashboard, so the audience count
shown is the count that gets messaged, with a per-reason breakdown of who is
held back.

**Design notes worth keeping**

- Broadcasts are always WATI templates WhatsApp has marked `APPROVED`. Outside a
  24-hour customer-initiated window WhatsApp silently drops non-template
  messages, so a free-text composer would report a successful send of ~1 500
  messages that nobody received.
- WhatsApp has no link-click webhook. A broadcast whose parameter contains
  `{{link}}` gets a per-recipient tracked link through `api/wa-click.js`, which
  is how "who clicked" is answered. The redirect is public by design (the clicker
  has no admin session) and is not an open redirect — the destination comes from
  the broadcast row, not the request, and is https-guarded.
- Phone normalization was written against the real column, which holds
  `0821234567`, `27821234567`, `270821234567`, `821234567`, `0027821234567`,
  Cape Town landlines, and fields containing two numbers. Ambiguous values are
  reported to the admin as unusable rather than guessed at and messaged.
- Delivery webhooks arrive out of order, so status only ever moves forward
  (queued → sent → delivered → read) and the analytics funnel is recomputed from
  the recipient rows rather than read off the broadcast's counters.
- Opt-outs are captured from a STOP-style reply, from a customer switching
  broadcasts off inside WhatsApp (read back by the sync's pull half), and from an
  admin. The first opt-out keeps its original timestamp — a later duplicate reply
  must not overwrite a compliance answer.
